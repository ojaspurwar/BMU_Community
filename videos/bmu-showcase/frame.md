# Design Spec: BMU Community (CampusPulse OS)

## Brand Identity & Palette
- **Canvas Base:** `#06090e` (Deep Void Slate)
- **Card Surface:** `#0f172a` (Frosted Dark Navy)
- **Card Highlight Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Accent Primary (Blue):** `#2563eb` (Sapphire Blue • Telemetry & System)
- **Accent Success (Green):** `#10b981` (Mint Green • Marketplace & Active State)
- **Accent Urgent (Red):** `#ef4444` (Crimson Red • HackBMU 7.0 & Events)
- **Accent AI (Purple/Cyan):** `#6366f1` / `#38bdf8` (Pulse AI Engine)
- **Text Primary:** `#f8fafc`
- **Text Muted:** `#94a3b8`

## Typography
- **Display Headings:** `Outfit`, sans-serif (font-weight: 800, letter-spacing: -0.03em)
- **Body & Subtitles:** `Inter`, sans-serif (font-weight: 500, letter-spacing: -0.01em)
- **Badges & Numbers:** `Inter`, monospace/tabular-nums (font-weight: 700)

## Layout & Motion Style
- **Theme:** Ultra-clean Dark Mode with frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- **Transitions:** Smooth fade-in section titles with floating card transitions, subtle 3D scale zooms (`scale: 1.04`), and energetic badge glows.
- **Duration:** 15 seconds (4 key scene beats).
